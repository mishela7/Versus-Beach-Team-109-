About Us

Versus Beach Bar is a modern spot on the shores of Lake Ohrid in Struga, created for everyone who loves good food, a pleasant atmosphere, and unforgettable moments with family and friends.

Here, you can enjoy a diverse menu, refreshing cocktails, coffee, desserts, and specialties while relaxing with a beautiful view of the lake.
